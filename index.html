About Me关于我

My research focuses on Neural Operators and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), aiming to develop efficient data-driven methods for solving partial differential equations. I am always open to collaborations and discussions — feel free to reach out! 我的研究方向是神经算子（Neural Operators）与物理信息神经网络（PINNs）， 致力于开发高效的数据驱动方法求解偏微分方程。欢迎随时联系交流合作！

PINN
